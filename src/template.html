Quick-start flow
1. Choose Species, Career/Specialization, free skill ranks, and spend creation XP.
2. Finalize from Review. Creation characteristics lock; earned XP becomes advancement XP.
3. Play Episode 0 from Adventure. The game previews the best party member and dice pool before most checks.
4. Use Recovery after fights, Equipment for loadout/workshop, and Crew for companion progression.
5. Finish Hangar Twelve to acquire the ship and unlock ship operations.

Narrative dice reminder
Ability / Proficiency produce Success and Advantage; Difficulty / Challenge produce Failure and Threat. Net Success determines whether the check works. Advantage/Threat describe side effects. Triumph/Despair can trigger major consequences even when the basic check succeeds or fails.

In combat, use Aim, movement, cover, qualities, Destiny, and symbol-spending before ending the turn.

Relationship thresholds

Recovery & Medical Treatment

Treat wounds, recover strain, and attempt to heal Critical Injuries between encounters.
Encounter
New day / full downtime reset
A full night's rest recovers 1 wound and all strain, and resets daily stimpack diminishing returns. Every five standard days of rest, the game makes the weekly natural Resilience check for one Critical Injury. Medicine treatment attempts are also limited per injury per standard week.
